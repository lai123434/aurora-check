I think the social skills thing is actually harder than it looks. When I did my internship last summer, my boss told me the thing she cared about most was not my GPA but whether I could talk to the client without making them angry. That surprised me a lot. I always thought good grades were enough. But she said a lot of students come in and they can do the spreadsheet very well, and then in the meeting they just sit there and say nothing. So the company has to teach them how to speak. I do not really know how to fix this in a course. Maybe group work helps a bit, but honestly most group work in my university is one person doing everything. I noticed that the students who are good at this usually did part time jobs or joined clubs, not because the class taught them.
