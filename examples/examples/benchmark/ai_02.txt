In today's rapidly evolving landscape of higher education, the cultivation of essential social skills has emerged as a pivotal concern for graduates seeking to thrive in the contemporary workplace. Employers consistently emphasise that effective communication, collaborative teamwork, and adaptive problem-solving constitute the cornerstone of professional success. Furthermore, the capacity to navigate interpersonal dynamics with nuance and empathy is increasingly regarded as a differentiating factor in competitive labour markets. Consequently, universities bear a responsibility to integrate structured opportunities for skill development throughout the curriculum. Moreover, students should proactively engage in extracurricular activities that foster genuine collaboration. In conclusion, the cultivation of social skills represents a multifaceted endeavour that demands sustained institutional commitment and individual agency.
